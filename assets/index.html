✨ About StreamSutra v1.1.0
StreamSutra v1.1.0 LATEST UPDATE Nov 2026
  • Dynamic Multi-Filter Dropdown Suite: Modernized category navigation into a fully dynamic dropdown filter bar directly above the channel grid. Dropdowns for Category, Language, Resolution/Quality, Country, and Sorting adapt 100% to whatever playlist is loaded.
  • Stream Quality & Resolution Auto-Detection: Automatic recognition of 4K / UHD, 1080p FHD, 720p HD, and SD streams with instant one-tap quality filtering.
  • Full-Width TV & Mobile Screen Optimization: Freed horizontal screen width by eliminating cramped category sidebars, maximizing channel card visibility and video player immersion.
  • Multi-Criteria Cascading Filter Engine: Filter channels simultaneously across search query, dynamic group, audio language, resolution, and country with live channel counts and one-tap ↺ Reset.
  • GZIP Support (.gz): Native streaming and decompression of compressed .xml.gz EPG guides and .m3u.gz playlists via Android HTTP bridge.
StreamSutra v1.0.0 INITIAL RELEASE Nov 2026
  • Bring-Your-Own-Content (BYOC): 100% open-source media player designed for user-provided IPTV links. Connect any Remote M3U URL, local .m3u file, Xtream Codes provider (Server/User/Pass), or XMLTV EPG schedule.
  • Exclusive Custom Fullscreen Controls: Complete elimination of browser media controls for exclusive custom HUD playback, one-tap play/pause, animated center flash indicator, and smooth swipe channel surfing.
  • Dual Multi-View Split Screen: Side-by-side (landscape) and stacked (portrait) 2-channel simultaneous playback with 1-tap audio focus toggle.
  • TV Ergonomics & Zapping: Quick-Flip recents tray (last 8 channels), instant numeric remote zapping (0–9 keypad with glowing OSD badge), and TV remote D-Pad navigation.
  • Multi-Audio & Subtitles Switcher: Native HLS.js multi-track audio stream and subtitle toggle.
  • Sleep Timer: Configurable 15m, 30m, 60m, 120m sleep timer with graceful 10-second audio fadeout and standby pause.
  • Lightweight & Ultra-Fast: Sub-500 KB standalone Android hybrid APK with zero-lag startup via browser IndexedDB caching.
